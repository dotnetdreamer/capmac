# The screen, in VS Code

The simulator's screen, or your Mac app's window, streams into a tab beside your code as smooth video.

- **Click and drag** to touch, **scroll** to scroll, **type** to type. Hold **Alt** and drag to pinch
- **Ctrl+Shift+H** is the Home button, **Ctrl+Shift+L** locks the screen
- **Ctrl+Shift+S** saves a screenshot and opens it
- A Mac app's dialogs show in the tab, so they never pop up on the Mac

Set **CapMac › Screen: Location** to `window` to give it a window of its own, like a real simulator.
