# Your app, found for you

Open your project's folder. CapMac looks for apps in it, and in the folders inside it:

| You have | CapMac runs |
| --- | --- |
| A Capacitor or Ionic app | its iPhone app, and its Electron app on the Mac |
| A Flutter app | its iPhone app, its Mac app and its Windows app |
| An Electron app | it, on the Mac's desktop, its window in VS Code |

It also recognises React Native, Expo, Tauri, .NET MAUI, Cordova and NativeScript apps, and says so in the **Apps** view.

When a folder has more than one app, pick the one to work on in the **Apps** view or the status bar.
